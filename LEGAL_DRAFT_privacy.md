## What we collect

When you create a member profile, we collect: your first and last name,
agency/company name, home state, licensed states, lines of business,
specializations, a short bio, an optional Facebook profile URL, and an
optional profile photo. Your email address and password (or Google
account, if you sign in that way) are collected at signup and managed by
our authentication provider, Supabase.

## How we use it

Your profile information is shown to other signed-in members in the
directory so they can find licensed referral partners. Basic and Pro
members' profiles additionally show a contact link to other members; Pro
members can receive direct messages from other members through the
site's built-in messaging feature. We do not sell your information to
third parties.

## Payment information

If you subscribe to a paid membership tier, your payment is processed
directly by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number —
Stripe handles that. We do store your subscription tier and Stripe
customer/subscription identifiers so we can keep your account status in
sync with your billing status.

## Email

We use Resend to send account-related email: welcome messages, new
message notifications (which you can turn off from your profile), and
billing status confirmations (activation, plan changes, cancellation —
these never include a dollar amount and cannot be turned off, since
they're tied to your account, not marketing). You can also manage
Supabase's built-in account emails (confirmation, password reset)
through the same provider.

## Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember a pending checkout
selection if you start upgrading before finishing your profile, so you
don't have to re-select it after completing signup. This is cleared
automatically after use or after 2 hours, and also on logout. We do not
use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
